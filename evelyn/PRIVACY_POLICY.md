# Privacy Policy — Evelyn

**Application:** Evelyn  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-15

## Summary

Evelyn stores your imported data, including LinkedIn connections and conversations, and the relationship records it creates on your own system. We do not retain a copy of your Evelyn data.

## Connected Services

When you connect an account, Evelyn retrieves the data you authorize from that service. If you configure an external AI provider, tool, or sharing connection, it may receive data needed to carry out the tasks you enable. That service's privacy policy applies to the data it receives.
